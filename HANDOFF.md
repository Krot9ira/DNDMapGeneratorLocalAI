# HANDOFF: Critical Bug Analysis - Wall Hallucinations and Layout Deviation

## 1. Executive Summary

- status: **CRITICAL BUG (UNRESOLVED)**
- **Issue:** The rendered battle map images generated by Ideogram 4 consistently deviate from the structural grid scheme (preview.png / map.json):
  1. **Hallucinated Walls on Open Ground:** Plazas and squares (e.g. Fountain Square) receive enclosing parapets, stone railings, or terrace walls where the scheme has open floor.
  2. **Hallucinated Corner Buildings:** Empty open street areas (e.g. bottom-right quadrant of burning_quarter) receive unwanted building facades/ruins.
  3. **Unwanted Interior Room Subdivisions:** Single undivided buildings (e.g. Stone House) receive internal partition walls dividing them into multiple cubicles.
  4. **Perimeter Enclosures:** Outdoor open scenes receive boundary walls along the bleed margin.

---

## 2. Technical Root Cause Analysis

### A. Fundamental Model Limitation (Pure Text-to-Image Diffusion)
- The generator relies on **Ideogram 4** running via ComfyUI.
- Ideogram 4 is a **pure Text-to-Image (T2I)** diffusion model. It accepts text prompts and soft cross-attention bounding boxes (compositional_deconstruction.elements with normalized coordinates [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax]).
- **Ideogram 4 has NO spatial image guidance**:
  - No ControlNet (Canny, Depth, LineArt, Segmentation).
  - No inpainting masks for structural walls.
  - No raster grid conditioning.

### B. Ideogram 4's Prior Bias vs. Text Constraints
- Ideogram 4's pretraining dataset on fantasy tabletop maps heavily associates RPG maps with:
  - Enclosed square compounds.
  - Subdivided multi-room structures.
  - Balanced street corners with buildings on all four edges.
- Even when the text prompt explicitly specifies:
  - *"There are exactly 3 buildings in this picture... and no other building or wall exists anywhere on this map. Every other part of the picture is completely open, unbroken ground with no walls, no buildings, and no partitions."*
  - *"This is an open-air outdoor site under open sky with no overhead cover."*
  - *"It is a single undivided space filling the whole of this rectangle from side to side with no interior wall and no partition."*
- The model's internal diffusion latent trajectory overrides the text instructions and generates walls, balustrades, and partitions.

---

## 3. What Was Attempted and Systemic Improvements Made

1. **Elimination of Open-Ground Bounding Boxes:**
   - Problem: Previous pipeline versions emitted bounding boxes for open_ground tiles, Fountain Square, and Burning Street. Ideogram treated any bounding box as a physical container and drew walls around it.
   - Fix: In tools/ideogram_prompt.py and app/include/ideogram_caption.h, bounding box emission for open outdoor spaces was eliminated when enclosure == 'open'.
2. **Open-Air Viewpoint Prompt Separation:**
   - Problem: styles/_base.json contained "The roof of this place has been lifted away... what is shown is the top edges of the walls", which caused outdoor maps to be interpreted as roofless buildings with perimeter walls.
   - Fix: Added viewpoint_note_open for outdoor scenes ("This is an open-air outdoor site under open sky with no overhead cover. Nothing stands over the open ground.").
3. **Suppression of 1x1 Prop Bounding Boxes inside Single Rooms:**
   - Problem: Generic room clutter (tables, crates, barrels) placed inside single rooms were given individual 1x1 bounding boxes, triggering Ideogram to build walls/compartments around them.
   - Fix: Generic unlabelled props inside single rooms are moved to loose clutter text and omitted from elements.
4. **Parity and Validation:**
   - Full caption parity between Python and C++ was maintained and passes all test suites (check_scenes.py, check_captions.py, check_caption_parity.py, check_layouts.py).

---

## 4. Current State and Remaining Failure Modes

- Despite stripping all conflicting bounding boxes and using strict negative/positive text constraints:
  - battlemap_00122_.png (Rendered from burning_quarter.json):
    - Fountain Square received a stone balustrade / parapet on its perimeter.
    - South-east corner received an unrequested building ruin.
    - Stone House still received a horizontal internal division.
- **Conclusion:** Prompt engineering and bounding-box filtering have reached their theoretical ceiling. Text+bounding box conditioning alone cannot enforce strict 1:1 pixel/tile-level wall fidelity in Ideogram 4.

---

## 5. Recommended Next Steps for Future Development

To achieve true 1:1 structural fidelity where walls on the scheme are guaranteed to match the render:

1. **ControlNet / Spatial Conditioning Integration:**
   - Introduce a diffusion backend (or secondary model/adapter) that supports hard spatial control (e.g. FLUX/SDXL with ControlNet LineArt/Tile/Depth, or a dedicated map ControlNet).
   - Rasterize TileGrid walls into a high-contrast line-art mask and feed it as a ControlNet tensor into the sampler.
2. **Two-Stage Composite Rendering:**
   - Stage 1: Render ground textures and props via diffusion.
   - Stage 2: Composite / inpaint the exact geometric walls and door thresholds on top of the rendered ground using map_rasterizer / blend shaders.
3. **Explore New Fine-Tuned Map Models:**
   - Train a LoRA or fine-tune an open diffusion model specifically on paired (grid_schema, rendered_battlemap) datasets with layout mask inputs.
